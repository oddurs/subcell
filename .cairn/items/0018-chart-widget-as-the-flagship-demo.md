## Problem

The claim that needs a demonstration is that a `subcell` chart looks
categorically better than a naive one at the *same* glyph tier. A chart is the
honest test because curves, thin strokes and gradients all fail visibly.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Line, area and sparkline
- [ ] Uses coverage antialiasing, not glyph selection alone
- [ ] Side-by-side capture against a whole-cell renderer at the same tier
